    How the numbers are made

    Every CO₂ and fuel figure in AetherX is an estimate. Here is the arithmetic, so you can check it.

    1 · Per-segment emissions

    Each route from OSRM comes split into steps, each with a length and an expected duration. From those we get the average speed of every step and apply:

    CO₂(step) = distance_km × base_factor × speed_curve(v) × stop_penalty

    speed_curve is U-shaped with its minimum around 60 km/h: crawling traffic and high motorway speeds both cost more per km. stop_penalty adds 4–18% on short urban steps where stop-start driving is likely.

    Speed curve used

    2 · Traffic

    OSRM's times assume empty roads. We stretch each step by how congested roads of that kind typically get at the hour you pick. This is a modelled pattern, not live traffic.

    Free-flow speed of roadPeakDaytimeNight
    Under 25 km/h (side streets)×1.35×1.15×1.00
    25–45 km/h (city arterials)×1.95×1.35×1.00
    45–70 km/h (main roads)×1.60×1.25×1.00
    Over 70 km/h (highways)×1.15×1.05×1.00

    3 · Picking the eco route

    We ask OSRM for alternatives and rank them by estimated CO₂. The fastest route is always shown next to it with the time difference. The eco route is a suggestion; you choose.

    4 · Charging stops (EV)

    Energy = CO₂ model ÷ grid factor. If the trip would drop the battery under 15%, we find the point where charge reaches about 20% and look up charging stations within 6 km of it from OpenStreetMap (Overpass API).

    5 · What we do with your data

    • Location: only read if you press ◎, and the browser asks you first. It is not stored.
    • Trips: kept in this browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded. “Clear my data” deletes it.
    • Voice: speech recognition is done by your browser. We only see the text of the command.
    • Third parties: place names go to Nominatim (geocoding), coordinates to OSRM (routing) and Overpass (chargers), under their usage policies.

    Factors are rounded from published averages (India petrol ≈ 2.31 kg CO₂/L, diesel ≈ 2.68 kg CO₂/L, grid ≈ 0.71 kg CO₂/kWh). Real figures vary with load, AC use, tyres and driving style.
